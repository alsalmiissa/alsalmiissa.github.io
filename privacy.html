Privacy policy

YNotes doesn't collect anything about you. Your notes stay on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on sync.

Effective 24 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your notes, the PDFs and slides you import, your flashcards, calendar and settings are stored on your device. YNotes has no server and no account of its own. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer receives none of your data.

Google Drive

If you sign in to Google Drive, YNotes asks only for the drive.file permission: access to the files YNotes creates, not to anything else in your Drive. It uses this to keep a copy of your notes, flashcards and study calendar in an xnotes folder in your Drive, and to read them back on your other devices. The sign-in key Google gives YNotes is stored on your device only. You can sign out in YNotes' settings, or remove YNotes' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YNotes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to sync your notes, isn't shared with anyone, and isn't used for advertising or to train AI models.

The study partner

The study partner uses an AI service only when you ask it something, and only with your own API key. It sends that service the question and the parts of your note it needs: page text, and pictures of the pages you ask about. By default the service is OpenRouter. If you set up your own relay in settings, requests may go through that relay instead. What happens to that data is governed by those services' own policies.

Claude, Gemini and the web panel

The side panel shows claude.ai, gemini.google.com or other websites in a browser inside the app, signed in with your own accounts. Those sites' own privacy policies apply to what you do there. Their cookies are kept on your device, so you stay signed in. When you attach a page or a PDF there, it's sent to that site because you chose to. If a site asks to use the microphone or camera (for a voice chat), Android asks you first, and you can refuse.

Files from other apps

When you open or share a PDF or slide deck with YNotes, it's copied into the app to make a note, and the temporary copy is then deleted.

Children

YNotes isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen when you sign in from YNotes.
