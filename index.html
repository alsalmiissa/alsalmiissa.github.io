YNotes

A notes app for Android tablets and phones: write by hand on PDFs and PowerPoint slides, and study from them.

  • Write on your material. PDFs and slide decks become notes you write on with a stylus or a finger.
  • Study. A study partner, quizzes, flashcards and a focus timer, built from your own notes.
  • Claude and Gemini beside the page, signed in with your own accounts.
  • Your own Google Drive. Notes can sync to a folder in your Drive, so they're on all your devices.

Google Drive

YNotes asks Google for access only to the files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It can't see any other file in your Drive. Your notes go straight between your device and your Drive; they never pass through anyone else's server.

Privacy policy

YNotes is a personal project. Questions: use the support email shown when you sign in to Google from YNotes.
